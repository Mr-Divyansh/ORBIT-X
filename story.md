            # 🌙 Aadhi Raat Ka Mehmaan

Abhay aur Divyansh ne is baar kuch naya try karne ki thaani — chhat par tent laga kar raat bitaana. Abhay ke papa ne purana tent nikaal diya, Divyansh torch aur biscuit ka pura packet utha laaya, aur shaam hote-hote dono apni "camping site" taiyaar karne mein jut gaye.

Raat nau baje tak sab set tha — sleeping bags, torch, aur ek purani kahaaniyon wali kitaab jo Divyansh zid karke saath laaya tha.

"Ek sacchi daravni kahani sunaun?" usne phusfusate hue poocha.

"Sacchi wali bilkul nahi!" Abhay ne kambal aur upar tak khinch liya. "Pichli baar teri kahani ke baad mujhe teen din light jalakar sona pada tha."

Par Divyansh kahan maanne wala tha. Torch thuddi ke neeche rakhi — bilkul waise hi jaise har daravni kahani mein hota hai — aur shuru ho gaya.

Aadhi kahani bhi poori nahi hui thi ki chhat ke kone se kuch khurachne jaisi aawaz aayi.

Dono ek pal ke liye jam gaye.

"Woh... woh kya tha?" Abhay ne dheere se poocha, uski aawaz achanak bahut chhoti ho gayi.

Aawaz phir aayi — is baar thodi tez, jaise koi cheez tent ki deewar ko kheench rahi ho.

Divyansh ne torch band kar di. "Shayad nahi dekhna chahiye."

"Shayad dekhna hi chahiye," Abhay ne kaha — par hila tak nahi.

Kheenchtaan phir hui, aur is baar tent ka ek kona sach mein hil gaya. Dono ek-dusre se sat kar baith gaye, saans rokkar.

Phir tent ki zip dheere-dheere khulne lagi.

"Bhaag!" Divyansh chillaya — par bhaage kahan, dono toh ek-dusre ki baanh pakde wahin jame reh gaye.

Zip poori khuli, aur andar jhaanki... ek geeli naak, do badi-badi udaas aankhein, aur ek dum jo zor-zor se hil rahi thi.

"...Moti?" Abhay ne kaanpte haathon se torch jalaayi.

Waakai Moti tha — Abhay ke ghar ka chhota pilla — jo neeche se kisi tarah seedhiyan chadhkar, raaste mein padosi ki sookhti hui chadar mein ulajhkar, pura raasta khud ko bina jaane "bhoot" banate hue yahan tak pahunch gaya tha. Uski peeth par ab bhi aadhi chadar latak rahi thi.

Ek second ki chuppi. Phir dono ek saath hans pade — itni zor se ki neeche se Abhay ki mummy ki aawaz aayi, "Kya hua upar?"

"Kuch nahi!" dono ne ek saath chillakar jawab diya. "Bas... Moti hai."

Moti beech mein ghuskar dono ke sleeping bags ke beech gol hokar baith gaya — jaise uski jagah toh hamesha se yahin thi.

Divyansh ne biscuit ka ek tukda uski taraf badhaya. "Agli baar se tujhe bhi invite karna padega, pata hai?"

Moti ne bas dum hilaayi — jaise use pehle se hi pata tha.

Us raat teeno wahin so gaye: do dost, ek "bhoot", aur aadhi chadar jo ab takiya ban chuki thi.
