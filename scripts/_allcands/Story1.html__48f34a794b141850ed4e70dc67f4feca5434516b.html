01

The Dark Doorstep

Old quarter · Halloween night

The old quarter was quiet on Halloween night — too quiet for Abhay and Divyansh. Every house they passed had curtains drawn and porch lights off, as if the whole street had quietly decided not to celebrate.

Divyansh hugged his magic staff and frowned at the darkness ahead.

Divyansh: “No lights means no treats. No treats means no Halloween for the kids down here.”

Abhay: “Then we bring the Halloween to them.”

He lifted his lantern staff high, and its warm glow spilled across the pavement like a door being opened. Somewhere behind a curtain, a small face pressed closer to the glass — and for the first time that night, it smiled.

02

The First Lantern

The corner house

Their first stop was the house at the corner. A small face peeked from behind the curtain — a boy in a t-shirt costume that was clearly meant to be something braver.

Abhay knelt down and held out a carved pumpkin, its smile glowing from within. The boy stared at it as if it were a small sun that had wandered indoors.

Divyansh: “Happy Halloween.”

The boy smiled so wide it looked like his costume had finally come true. He took the pumpkin in both hands, and when he stepped outside with it, the porch light above him flickered on all by itself.

By the time the friends left, three more lanterns were swinging from the porch rail — and the street was no longer dark.

03

A Trail Of Light

Midnight · looking back

Door after door, the trail grew. Kids who had never knocked on a neighbour's house started joining the line, carrying lanterns of their own, handing out treats as fast as they received them. The skipped street turned into the brightest one in the old quarter.

Abhay: “Every doorstep has one.”

Divyansh: “Then the night's complete.”

When the clock struck midnight, Abhay and Divyansh stood at the end of the street and looked back. It was a river of lanterns — and every single doorstep had a light.

Divyansh: “Best Halloween ever.”

Abhay: “Until next year.”

He was already planning the next mission, because the street behind them did not stay kind on its own — it stayed kind because somebody kept carrying lanterns. And lanterns, it turns out, are the kind of treat that multiplies when you give it away.

ONCE UPON A TIME

A quiet night.
A world of possibilities.

It began on an ordinary evening, when the sky turned a beautiful shade of violet and the first stars appeared. Somewhere beyond the familiar world, a mysterious little universe was about to reveal itself.

Abhay had always been curious about the things others overlooked. Strange lights, forgotten paths, and stories hidden between the pages of old books — nothing escaped his imagination.

“Sometimes, the greatest adventures begin with one small step into the unknown.”

When Divyansh joined the journey, the unknown no longer seemed quite so mysterious. Together, they discovered that curiosity was stronger than fear and friendship could make even the strangest places feel like home.
