# Image Generation Instructions

These instructions define the visual style and rules for generating assets for the ORBIT-X Halloween story website. Use them in a new ChatGPT conversation or another image-generation tool, along with the relevant reference images.

## 1. Overall visual direction
- Create cute, polished Halloween-fantasy assets that fit the ORBIT-X website.
- Keep the mood magical, friendly, playful, and slightly spooky—not scary or dark.
- Use the supplied reference images to match the established visual identity.
- Generate only the asset requested. Do not recreate the whole website unless explicitly asked.
- Do not add extra decorations or objects that were not requested.

## 2. Art style: 2D + soft 3D
- Use a **2D illustration with subtle, soft 3D / clay-like depth**.
- Keep shapes simple, rounded, smooth, and readable at small sizes.
- Use gentle highlights, soft shading, and very subtle shadows to suggest depth.
- Avoid highly realistic materials, sharp edges, heavy texture, or a fully photorealistic look.
- The result should feel like a clean, cute digital illustration with a light clay-inspired finish—not a bulky, fully 3D render.
- Maintain consistent lighting, proportions, outlines, and detail level across all assets.

## 3. Colour palette
- Prefer soft pastel colours: lavender, lilac, pale blue, warm cream, and gentle pink.
- Use pumpkin orange where appropriate, with muted green for stems and leaves.
- Use deep plum or dark purple for small facial features and outlines.
- Keep colours soft and harmonious with the ORBIT-X reference website.
- Avoid neon colours, harsh contrast, and overly saturated backgrounds unless specifically requested.

## 4. Asset requirements
Create separate, standalone assets when requested, such as:
- Pumpkins
- Cute ghosts
- Small bats
- Spiders and spider webs
- Stars and sparkles
- Bottom-corner clouds or decorative background elements

For each request:
- Follow the user's sketch or reference for the shape and pose.
- Preserve the requested direction, expression, and placement.
- Keep the subject fully visible unless the user specifically asks for it to extend beyond the canvas.
- Do not combine multiple requested assets into a sprite sheet unless asked.
- Do not add text, logos, UI, borders, or watermarks unless requested.

## 5. Background and transparency
- If the asset is meant to be layered over a website, prefer a **transparent background (PNG)**.
- If a solid background is specifically requested, use a pure white background (`#FFFFFF`).
- For a website background layer, create a wide desktop composition (typically 16:9) with ample empty space.
- Keep decorative elements near the requested edges or corners; leave the central content area clear.
- Do not add a checkerboard pattern to represent transparency. Use actual transparency when supported.
- Do not place unwanted clouds, stars, characters, or decorations in empty areas.

## 6. ORBIT-X website background rules
When creating the bottom-corner cloud background:
- Use a pure white canvas.
- Place one soft pastel cloud partly entering from the **bottom-left corner** and another partly entering from the **bottom-right corner**.
- The clouds should extend beyond the canvas edges so they feel cropped naturally by the viewport.
- Angle the left cloud slightly toward the right and the right cloud slightly toward the left.
- Keep the entire upper area and center blank white.
- Do not include characters, text, stars, sparkles, pumpkins, bats, ghosts, or any other decorations.
- The clouds should remain at the bottom corners and must not cover the central website content.
- The image is intended as a desktop background layer behind other website elements. Mobile visibility and responsive positioning will be handled separately in the website layout.

## 7. Individual character guidance

### Pumpkin
- Cute, rounded pumpkin with soft orange shading and a small natural green stem or leaf.
- Friendly Halloween face, with simple dark-plum features.
- Keep the shape readable and uncluttered.

### Ghost
- Cute floating ghost with a smooth, rounded sheet-like body and a wavy lower edge instead of legs.
- Friendly face; optional tiny leaf, vine, or pumpkin-inspired sprout on its head if requested.
- Use soft white/cream with lavender-blue shading.
- Keep any moustache-like detail subtle and cute if specifically requested.

### Bat
- Small, cute upright bat with rounded body, simple wings, and a playful mischievous grin.
- Add only a faint blush of red/pink on the cheeks if requested.
- Keep the pose upright and the wings balanced; do not rotate the character unless asked.

### Spider and spider web
- Small, friendly spider with a rounded body and simple face.
- Delicate lavender spiderweb lines, thin and tidy, matching the corner-web references.

## 8. Composition and consistency
- Match the scale and visual weight of the supplied reference assets.
- Keep all characters and decorative objects stylistically consistent as if they belong to the same asset pack.
- Use soft studio-like lighting and clean edges.
- Avoid unnecessary glow, excessive sparkles, busy backgrounds, or extra props.
- If the user asks for one asset only, generate one asset only.
