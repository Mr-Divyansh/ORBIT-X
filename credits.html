🎧 Music & Sound Effects

Seventeen ambient tracks power the ORBIT-X atmosphere — background music, night-time ambience, UI clicks, and magical scene transitions.

  • Music library: SoundHelix Songs ↗ — royalty-free, released for free use (CC0) by SoundHelix
  • 17 tracks stored locally in Assets/audio/ (music · ambience · ui · interactions · transitions)
  • Full track list & licenses: manifest.json · README.md
  • Playback engine: custom js/audioManager.js (no external audio library)
🔊 The bundled songs are full-length recordings. For production, consider trimming them into short loops (e.g. with ffmpeg).
