A LITTLE HALLOWEEN MAGIC

Every Magic Has a Story

Two unlikely friends, one magical mission, and a dream world waiting to be saved.

SCENE 01 ✦

Two Friends. One Magical Universe.

Abhay and Divyansh are two unlikely friends with one extraordinary purpose: to protect children from the frightening creatures hiding inside their nightmares.

With courage, kindness, and a little Halloween magic, they are always ready to step in when a dream needs a friend.

SCENE 02 ✦

Trick, Treat & a Little Mischief

On Halloween night, the friends wander through the neighborhood, where some grown-ups have forgotten to share the treats.

So the duo plans harmless, giggle-worthy tricks to remind everyone that Halloween is for kindness, fun, and making sure every child gets to join in.

SCENE 03 ✦

When Dreams Turn Scary

Then they discover that some children are carrying frightening dreams long after the night is over. Their playful Halloween adventure becomes something more important.

Now the friends will help young dreamers face their fears gently—and feel safe enough to dream again.

A Little Courage Changes Everything

Abhay lifts his magical lantern and reveals the hidden ghosts. Divyansh's twisted wand surrounds them with gentle magic and shows the way through the dream world.

Side by side, the friends turn frightening dreams into brave, beautiful adventures—one child, one kind moment, one little spark at a time.
