# Product Definition — ORBIT-X: Meet Momo

## 1. Product Name & Tagline
- **Product Name:** Orbit-X: The World of Momo
- **Tagline:** *"Weaving starlight into lanterns so no dream ever gets lost in the dark."*

---

## 2. Product Purpose
The purpose of this product is to deliver an unforgettable, warm, and highly engaging digital showcase for an original character, **Momo the Star-Weaver Baby**, created specifically for Track 2 of the haX.baby "Baby's First Website" Hackathon.

Rather than presenting a static portfolio with walls of text, this website invites visitors into an interactive storybook universe where character identity, lore, inventions, and playful interactions harmonize seamlessly.

---

## 3. The 5-Point Character Definition

### 1. Name
**Momo** (Known across the floating sky islands as *The Cloud Shepherd*).

### 2. Species, Appearance & Personality
- **Species:** Voxel Star-Sprite (a celestial toddler embodied from fallen comet dust and cloud foam).
- **Appearance:** A blocky, adorable voxel-style chibi character sitting comfortably on a puffy white cumulus cloud.
  - *Day Mode:* Dressed in a signature cozy honey-yellow and jet-black hoodie with warm golden eyes that sparkle like the sun.
  - *Night Mode:* Wrapped in a warm starlight fleece coat with midnight-blue galaxy eyes and a tiny glowing ghost hairclip.
- **Personality:** Sleepy, gentle, deeply curious, and protective of sleepy creatures. Momo loves sipping warm moon-milk and collecting bright ideas.
- **Voice / Quirk:** Speaks softly in cheerful rhymes and gentle whispers (*"A little spark makes every shadow soft!"*).

### 3. Profession & Special Talent
- **Profession:** Celestial Lantern Weaver & Dream Navigator at Orbit Sky-Station.
- **Special Talent:** Can spin stray cosmic rays into glowing amber lanterns that provide comfort and warmth to children and dreamers across the universe.

### 4. Origin Story & Bedtime Journey
Born during an astronomical convergence when a shy comet landed gently on a cloud pillow high above Orbit Castle. Finding himself with a heart full of light and a sky full of shadows, Momo began gathering starry sparks in woven brass lanterns to illuminate nighttime airways for sleepy wandering travelers.

### 5. Memorable Tagline
*"Weaving starlight into lanterns so no dream ever gets lost in the dark."*

---

## 4. Target Audience & User Personas
1. **Hackathon Judges:** Looking for seamless functionality, early-web nostalgic whimsy, responsive polish, and rich character personality.
2. **Casual Web Explorers:** Drawn to tactile UI moments, ambient audio chimes, interactive toys, and discoverable Easter eggs.
3. **Makers & Designers:** Appreciating the cohesive design tokens, warm cream-and-berry palette, and thoughtful micro-interactions.

---

## 5. MVP Scope Breakdown

### Must Have (Delivered in Initial Build)
- Character Hero stage with floating animation and dual CTA paths.
- Complete 5-point Character Sheet displayed in an editorial card format.
- 3 functional Invention cards with interactive exploration and live audio feedback.
- 3-scene scroll narrative under 150 words per scene.
- Dual Mood Switch: Instant visual swap between Golden Sunbeam and Starlight Dream modes.
- Hidden Easter Egg with particle confetti and celebratory modal/toast.
- Accessible Audio Toggle (mute/unmute with sound indicator).
- 100% mobile-friendly responsive layout.

### Bonus Enhancements (Included in Build)
- Canvas Sparkle Cursor Trail with stardust particle decay.
- Mechanical Hit Counter ticker with simulated persistent visit increments.
- Interactive Starlight Guestbook with local storage persistence.
- "Chaos Mode" vintage sticker overlay for maximum retro personality.

### Out of Scope (Deliberate Non-Goals)
- Bulky server-side database requirements that complicate zero-configuration hosting.
- Intrusive video backgrounds or heavy asset downloads that harm performance.
